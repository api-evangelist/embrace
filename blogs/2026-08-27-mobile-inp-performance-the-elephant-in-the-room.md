Earlier this year, when Google announced that Interaction to Next Paint (INP) will replace First Input Delay (FID) as the responsiveness metric in Core Web Vitals in *gulp* March of 2024, we had a lot to say about it . (TLDR: FID doesn’t correlate with real user behavior, so we don’t endorse it as a meaningful metric.) Our stance hasn’t changed much since then. For the most part, everyone agrees the transition from FID to INP is a good thing.
